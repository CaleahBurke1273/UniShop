ABOUT STUDENTMARKET

Made for student life

Find affordable essentials and sell the items you no longer need. StudentMarket connects university students across Canada with buyers and sellers in their school communities and nearby areas.

GETTING STARTED

A simple way to buy and sell

Shopping for essentials

Compare the price, condition, and pickup area before arranging a purchase. For textbooks, check the edition and course requirements so you can find the right book for your class.

Preparing a listing

Use clear photos and an accurate description. Include the item's condition, price, and general pickup area so other students know what to expect.

HERE TO HELP

Frequently asked questions

Who is StudentMarket for?

StudentMarket is designed for university students across Canada who want to buy and sell items within their school communities and nearby areas.

What can I find on StudentMarket?

Explore furniture, textbooks, electronics, school supplies, and other everyday essentials for student life. Each item card shows its price, condition, and location.

What details should I include in a listing?

Include a clear title, photos, price, condition, and an accurate description. Add your school or general pickup area, and mention any wear, damage, or missing parts.
